# Spin a Soccer Card Wiki

[Spin a Soccer Card Wiki](https://spinasoccercard.com) is an independent, player-built resource for the Roblox game **Spin a Soccer Card**. It helps players compare cards, understand pack progression, find recently reported codes, prepare for rebirths, and make better trading decisions.

The wiki separates official information, dated in-game observations, historical records, and unconfirmed reports so players can see how reliable each entry is. Because the game changes regularly, always compare older records with the current in-game Shop, Index, and card labels.

## About Spin a Soccer Card

Spin a Soccer Card is a collection and progression game built around opening soccer card packs. Players buy or earn packs, reveal cards of different ratings and rarities, place their best cards on a plot, and generate Cash over time.

Cash can be reinvested into stronger packs and better cards. Mutations may change a card's appearance or performance, while rebirths reset parts of your progress in exchange for permanent benefits and access to later content. Players can also redeem codes, use the Card Bank, collect Spin Wheel rewards, and trade cards in the Trade World.

## Game Features

- **Card collecting:** Open packs and build a collection of soccer cards with different ratings, rarities, income records, and mutations.
- **Pack progression:** Move through Shop tiers and compare pack costs, availability, rewards, and historical rotations.
- **Passive income:** Equip cards on your plot to earn Cash while playing or waiting between upgrades.
- **Mutations:** Discover visual variants and documented multipliers, while checking the live Index for current effects.
- **Rebirth progression:** Reset Cash and plot cards to unlock permanent boosts, milestones, and higher-level content.
- **Codes and free rewards:** Test community codes for packs, spins, Cash, or other limited rewards.
- **Trading:** Visit the Trade World, compare card offers and Trade Token amounts, and review every item before accepting.
- **Card Bank:** Protect selected cards before a rebirth when the relevant bank feature is available in your current progression.

## Beginner Guide

1. Keep every available plot slot filled so your cards continue producing Cash.
2. Compare the live income shown above cards instead of judging them by rarity alone.
3. Save Cash for the next useful Shop tier rather than spending everything on low-level packs.
4. Open the in-game Odds panel before making a large purchase.
5. Redeem current codes and collect free spins or packs whenever they are available.
6. Check mutation labels carefully because a mutated card may outperform an unmutated card with a higher rarity.
7. Before rebirthing, read the current reset warning and move important cards into the Card Bank when possible.
8. During trades, inspect every card, mutation, and Trade Token amount before confirming the offer.

For a more detailed walkthrough, visit the [Beginner Guide](https://spinasoccercard.com/guides/beginner-guide).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main goal of the game?

The main loop is to collect stronger cards, equip them on your plot, earn more Cash, unlock better packs, and progress through rebirths and other late-game systems.

### How do I get better cards?

Keep your plot producing Cash, progress to stronger Shop packs, check pack odds, redeem active codes, and collect free rewards. A useful mutation can also make an existing card more valuable to your setup.

### What is the best card?

The answer can change after updates. Compare the live income label, rating, mutation, and current Index information rather than relying only on an old tier list.

### How do mutations work?

Mutations are variants applied to cards. Some have documented effects or multipliers, while others may only have dated or incomplete records. Check the current in-game Index and the wiki's verification label before comparing them.

### What happens when I rebirth?

A rebirth resets selected progress, including Cash and plot cards, in exchange for permanent benefits and access to later milestones. Always read the current in-game rebirth panel because requirements and rewards can change.

### How can I protect cards before rebirthing?

Use the Card Bank when it is available to you and confirm that the cards are stored before accepting the rebirth reset. Bank access and capacity may depend on your current progression or purchased upgrades.

### Where can I find new codes?

Codes are commonly announced through the game's community channels and shown in player reports. The [Codes page](https://spinasoccercard.com/codes) tracks sightings and clearly separates recently verified, reported, and expired entries.

### Are card values and trade prices official?

No. Player trades can vary, and the game does not provide a universal fixed market price for every card. The [Values page](https://spinasoccercard.com/values) presents dated observations and available income records as reference points, not guaranteed prices.

### Is this the official game website?

No. Spin a Soccer Card Wiki is an independent, fan-made website and is not affiliated with Roblox, Pixellar Studios, or the game's developers.
